# 🏥 TeleHealth Nabha – SIH 2025 Project

**Smart India Hackathon 2025 | Software Edition**  
**Problem Statement ID:** 25018  
**Title:** Telemedicine Access for Rural Healthcare in Nabha

## 📌 Overview

TeleHealth Nabha is a digital health initiative aimed at transforming rural healthcare delivery in Nabha, Punjab. By leveraging telemedicine, we enable remote consultations, digital prescriptions, and health record management—designed specifically for low-bandwidth environments and underserved communities.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Rural regions like Nabha face critical challenges in accessing timely and quality healthcare due to:

- Limited medical infrastructure
- Shortage of qualified doctors
- Poor internet connectivity
- Lack of digital literacy

Our solution bridges this gap by creating a robust, user-friendly telemedicine platform that connects patients, ASHA workers, and doctors—anytime, anywhere.

## 💡 Features

- 📹 **Video & Audio Consultations**  
  Secure, low-data teleconsultation between patients and doctors

- 📝 **E-Prescription System**  
  Doctors can issue prescriptions digitally and instantly

- 📁 **Health Records Dashboard**  
  Patients and health workers can view and manage medical history

- 🌐 **Offline Mode & Sync**  
  Works even in areas with intermittent internet access

- 🔒 **Secure & Compliant**  
  End-to-end encryption and NDHM compliance
